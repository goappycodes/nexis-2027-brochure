Degree &
Certification

On completing all three years, students receive a Professional Certificate for the Undergraduate Programme in Business Management from NEXIS School of Business.

Students may independently pursue a UGC-recognised online Bachelor of Business Administration in parallel with NEXIS. Choose and enrol in a university that suits your goals.

NEXIS awards certification and does not confer a degree. The optional BBA degree is awarded by your chosen university under its separate admission and enrolment requirements.
